0.0.7 (pre-release)

New:
- Experimental Browser on Home, with its own WebView profile. Agent control is off by default and needs a new grant each time.
- Local OpenCode can use six browser tools through a local MCP server: navigate, snapshot, click, type, scroll and capture. Public https only.

Notes:
- The on-device OpenCode, MCP and WebView loop on an arm64 phone is not yet verified end to end.
